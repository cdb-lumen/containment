# Independent art review: Room8 stage4 attempt909

Verdict: failed.

## Evidence and scope

Inspected `desktop-in-scene.png`, `overview.png`, `before/desktop-in-scene.png` and `before/overview.png` with vision_analyze. Also inspected a crop of the candidate desktop seal and growth. This is a static art review. HUD, mobile, collision and live combat are outside the verdict. No source edits, rendering, GPU work or publishing were performed.

## Findings

- The rejected panels on rods are gone. The candidate replaces their tall, angular silhouette with a continuous low green mound along the diagonal seal. This is a real improvement in physical plausibility, but it does not establish convincing alien growth.
- The growth reads as a smooth strip of green putty laid on metal. Its broad surface has little structure beyond a few soft humps. There are no readable branching roots, attachment lobes, torn tissue or smaller growth forms that explain how it grips the scar. The continuous outline looks placed on the plate rather than grown from damage. This is the main acceptance failure.
- The boarding scar is not legible beneath or alongside the growth. Metal fragments and perimeter clamps imply a repair, but the center reads as green material on a largely regular ribbed plate. Alignment with the diagonal is visible; growth following a damaged seam is not. Preserve a visible damaged edge or interrupted seam beside the organic attachment rather than relying on the overall direction alone.
- At room scale, the growth becomes one broad, almost featureless green band. Its model quality is weaker than the surrounding clamps, broken metal and cargo. Replacing flat panels with a soft continuous silhouette has removed the prior construction error without supplying enough organic form.

## Physical contact and composition

The candidate appears seated on the seal. Neither current view establishes an obvious floating gap or severe clipping defect. The shadow behind the raised ridge is not sufficient evidence of floating. Exact mesh contact remains unverified, and lack of visible roots is a readability failure rather than proof of disconnected geometry.

The metal seal appears continuous and closed. No open hole or exterior exposure is visible, so the intact-hull requirement is visually preserved. The overview retains a clear central diagonal focal area with open floor around it. This supports the composition of a fight around a sealed breach, but static images do not verify traversal or combat.

## Required art correction

Keep the closed seal and low attached mass. Give the growth a readable origin at the boarding scar, uneven rooted contact and a small number of distinct organic forms that survive the desktop camera. Expose enough damaged seam to show what it follows. Do not return to separate panels on stalks, and do not treat a smoother silhouette alone as acceptance.
