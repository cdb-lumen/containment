# Coolant plant story intent

Keep the coolant plant working. The change is the player's knowledge after the exposed lie, not a plant failure or an already active purge.

Canonical objective: "Descend through the coolant pumps, knowing the fatal cost."

Two paired heat-exchanger installations establish coolant exchange and ongoing life support. Turquoise enamel, stainless plumbing and amber valve indicators distinguish machinery roles. Later layout work must retain figure-eight circulation, a low service saddle and clear central crossing. Decorative condensation stays on equipment; there is no slippery floor, liquid hazard or valve interaction.

This board is a stage0 conceptual diagram. It does not propose dimensions or approve layout, construction, combat, integration or release. No unaccepted preceding-room assets are used. No runtime files changed.
