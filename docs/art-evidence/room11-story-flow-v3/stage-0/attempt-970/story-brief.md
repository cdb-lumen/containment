# Diagnostic gallery story intent

Stage0 concept, attempt970. Refs #32. Not gameplay or a validated layout.

## Story

Original function: engineers inspect the ship from a semicircular service theatre.
After the war warning, the independent physical service display supplies consequence evidence: "Purge destroys every deck, including occupied cryopods."
Read the display while clearing the gallery. Passengers remain alive. Purge is not activated here. Skip remains immediate and never grants consent. Room12, not this room, reveals the pre-awakening record.

## Proposed physical idea

A shallow mechanical ship cutaway exposes stacked deck plates and a hardwired amber purge bus behind a partial cover. Occupied cryo symbols remain distinct from the amber consequence trace. Curved operator consoles face the board with low near-side backs and interrupted banks. Engineering teal and aged ivory separate housings from instruments. No arena-wide hologram or new interaction.

The concept simplifies deck count and console shape. It is neither ship engineering documentation nor a tested room plan. No unaccepted Room9/10 assets are inherited.

## Review and next step

Independent and parent review pass story intent only. The board's "reaches every deck" wording is weaker than the canonical destruction statement above. Preserve explicit fatal consequence in subsequent board design; do not make the amber route look like ordinary power distribution.

Next is layout against the existing entry/exit and actual geometry, then rough models. Runtime readability, collision, combat and all release tests remain unverified. No human room acceptance, merge or deployment.
