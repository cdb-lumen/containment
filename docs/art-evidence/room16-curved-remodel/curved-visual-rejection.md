## Findings

- **P2 — prominent triangular surface streaks undermine the carapace.** Both new native views show long, dense triangular fans across the west root and smaller fan seams across the east/south branches. They read as mesh tessellation/shading artifacts, not intentional layered plates or long coherent tendon structure. They remain apparent in overview. The previous smooth platform lacked these streaks. Investigate the heightfield triangulation/normal construction before further palette work: `src/render/organicShell.ts:11–18` retains original triangle edges and repeatedly splits faces by centroid, producing long fan triangles, then computes normals at line34. This is a plausible source-level contributor, not a proved exclusive rendering cause. Keep the validated contour/collision contract while improving interior tessellation and deliberate surface construction; preserve closed/manifold/containment controls. A new native image is required to establish the result.
- **P2 — the overall mass and cut ends still do not read as the specified organic manifold.** Curved, swollen outer ends and a broader asymmetric center remove much of the straight platform edge. Three route directions and the recessed sensory cavity are clear. However, the dominant reading remains a single broad Y-shaped molded fixture: two similar long arms, a short centered stem, a uniform raised edge and one continuous surface. Three pale terminal patches appear as small plates placed on top, not distinct exposed cross-sections of severed roots. The requirement is not to remove the three-root organization; it is to make that organization visibly biological through intentional layered overlap, directional tissue/ribs and irregular volumetric transitions. The contour revision alone has not achieved that.
- **P2 — human distributor identity/engulfment remains weak at shipping scale.** The larger pale housing is easier to locate, and its jagged lid outline differentiates it from the old thin bracket. In both native images it is still dominated by a nearly featureless pale face/top; connector/vent details are tiny and the dark face is a narrow lower strip. It reads as a cap attached to the Y apparatus, not a coherent damaged distribution cabinet whose recognizable human structure is being consumed. Mechanical contact and exposure-ray tests establish that geometry exists and is not fully occluded; they do not establish recognition. Strengthen visible enclosure depth, functional face contrast and a deliberate organic crossing over part of that enclosure. Do not merely enlarge the pale flat area again.

The far-wall directional gantry remains distinct, and the marine is visible in the two static poses. I found no evidence here that the three directions disappeared or that the native camera was changed to make the new shape appear better. Those positive observations do not waive the three visual findings.
