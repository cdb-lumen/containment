## Story and purpose

The chamber originally served the colony ship's communications system. The ship is now alien-seized. The player reaches this chamber after the relay racks and must defend the existing uplink sequence. This brief adds no new damage history, escape promise, shield, interaction or gameplay timing.

Pale ceramic vanes and brushed-alloy supports establish transmission equipment rather than a reactor. A perimeter truss carries the dish-feed segment. The sloped desk supports acknowledgment, but cannot replace existing essential status with tiny world text. Restrained blue-white light belongs to equipment, not a new protective field.

Before receipt, the player must understand that transmission is still underway. Only the actual milestone may show WARNING RECEIVED / NEW EARTH PREPARING FOR WAR. Fatal purge evidence remains in the following diagnostic gallery. No docking-clearance or evacuation message belongs here.
