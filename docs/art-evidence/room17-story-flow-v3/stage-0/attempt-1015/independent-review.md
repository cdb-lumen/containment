# Independent review of Room17 stage 0 attempt1015

Verdict: passed for story intent legibility and fidelity as an annotated stage 0 concept board. This is not model, room layout, gameplay or publication acceptance.

I inspected story-intent.png through the vision tool and read sourced-brief.md, rollout-sources/room17-brief.json and rollout-sources/storyRooms.ts. The image was presented at 1200 by 850 from the 2400 by 1700 original. This review is independent of the author verdict, but not blind to the supplied brief. I did not inspect a running game or independently fetch issue39.

## Story intent legibility

Passed. The objective at the top explicitly places this barrier at the last defensive line before the reactor. The right column preserves the defensive encounter with "Clear threats, then continue" rather than replacing combat with a gate-opening task. The central opening and the editorial arrow labeled "Toward reactor access" make the intended continuation clear. The board does not depict the reactor itself or imply that overload has started.

The gate reads as a heavy layered barrier. Stepped grey frames and inset leaves surround a strong central negative space. Steel-colored edges separate the layers. Threaded shafts are visible on both sides, connecting outer housings to ochre drive blocks. Small dosimeter plates reinforce the containment identity. The hardware stays beside the opening rather than spanning it. Nothing visible suggests a fallen leaf, breached frame, explosion or sabotage.

## Story fidelity

Passed. storyRooms.ts line 20 names Shielding gate, assigns the containment environment and gives the same objective as the board. Lines 21 through 23 reserve manual authorization and the overload sequence for later rooms. The board respects that order and explicitly excludes an overload countdown and escape cues.

room17-brief.json lines 12, 17 through 22 and 29 support static leaves, nested shielding, paired screw actuators, containment instruments and the grey, steel and ochre material treatment. The drawn assembly follows those directions at schematic detail. The text does not invent who opened the gate or why. Screw drives describe the construction without proposing a new interaction, timed closure or crushing hazard.

## Concrete limitations

The final defensive line is conveyed mainly by the objective and explanatory copy. There are no defenders, encounter staging or reactor-side spatial context in the drawing. The standalone assembly silhouette communicates a shielding gate, but cannot independently establish that this is the last defensive encounter. That is a limitation of this annotated concept, not a contradiction of its story.

The straight frontal aperture does not illustrate the stepped approach, staggered access or broad alternate lanes required by room17-brief.json lines 12 and 31. Those requirements survive in the board text and brief only. This pass must not be treated as evidence that a future room layout preserves them.

The paired shafts and nested layers are readable in this presentation. Their visibility at gameplay zoom, from the reverse approach or on a phone remains untested. Fine tongue-and-groove construction and compression seals are not independently legible here. The drawing gives schematic guides and wedges, not a mechanically verified assembly.

The teal arrow is legible as an editorial direction marker within this board. It does not establish an approved in-game sign or navigation cue. The blank dosimeter marks do not establish a radiation reading or hazard state.

The central gap is visually clear in this drawing only. No claim is made about traversability, projectile passage, collision, large-actor clearance, live combat or runtime gate state. The source brief is marked historical_revalidate_story_and_function_before_use. Its identity and objective agree with the supplied canonical story source, but this review does not replace later source revalidation or production acceptance.
