# Room6 carousel/horseshoe implementation review

The full original brief needs an explicit Room6-only topology revision. A genuine compact carousel could fit one existing island, but the four separated rectangles cannot honestly become a continuous curved horseshoe issue bay around it without changing either the collision contract or the requested form. Keeping the current topology is a valid intermediate milestone, not completion.

Read the current [Issue27 brief](https://github.com/cdb-lumen/containment/issues/27) through the GitHub connector and inspected current-main `57eba3b6544c769d9dc6f98c383903a036dadeb1` in `/workspace/containment-status-hud-current-baseline`. The brief explicitly requires a stationary sectorized rotary mechanism with spindle/geared base/radial clamps, camera-facing open sector, curved counter with trays/vise/hinged armor hangers, elite fight in the horseshoe mouth and broad outer retreat. Desktop-only scope supersedes old touch requirements; other substantive requirements remain.

## Fit assessment

Current Armory solids are `(280,180,280,100)`, `(740,180,180,100)`, `(280,600,180,100)` and `(740,530,180,170)` in the 1200×880 room. These are separated islands: guns northwest, armor northeast, straight issue bench southwest, ammunition southeast. The first three are only 100 units (3.125 render units) deep. Their existing full rectangular plinths deliberately own the blocked area.

A roughly 160-unit-diameter stationary carousel fits the southeast 180×170 island. It can have a true central spindle, bearing/geared foot and several angularly separated radial clamps carrying fixed-scale vertical rifles; south-facing sector stays open. This would be genuine rotary storage, but it would sit in a corner of the composition rather than inside a coherent horseshoe. Adding tiny curved counters to the other disconnected plinths or naming existing racks “carousel” would not resolve the composition. Bridging the islands with waist-height counters over legal floor would introduce invisible collision/visible obstruction disagreement. An overhead connector does not make an issue counter, and shallow floor markings alone do not satisfy the model brief.

## Recommended bounded candidate

Create a single Room6-owned layout module, used by both collision and assembly placement; preserve room bounds, spawn `(100,440)`, exit `(1100,440)`, four breach positions, encounter/reward behavior and all other templates. Keep the carousel stationary and decorative inventory inaccessible. No shared AI/projectile/camera changes or global lighting adjustment are needed.

Use a central rear carousel with a south-facing open sector, surrounded on north/west/east by a curved or deliberately faceted horseshoe issue counter. Keep the south mouth open for the elite fight and leave an outer circulation loop. As a starting reservation to evaluate, place the combined assembly within x300..900, y180..650, with the mouth facing south and roughly x450..750 clear. Reserve the carousel near `(600,350)` with diameter about160; counter inner edges must leave real passage between its rim and the counter. These are design envelopes, not validated final coordinates: derive actual polygons/segments and prove the largest production actor can traverse them before freezing the model.

Current blockers are rectangles. Prefer a small deliberate set of counter sectors with rectangular structural plinths and short faceted curved transitions whose actual solids agree with the same occupancy union. If a truly continuous curved counter cannot meet that contract, use the existing supported solid-polygon topology mechanism with explicit Room6-only data, after checking its navigation/shot semantics; do not pretend an AABB approximation with visibly empty blocked corners is exact. Do not add a new generic collision subsystem just for decorative curvature. The hollow horseshoe interior must remain ordinary traversable floor, not be classified as a floor hole.

Reuse the authored rifle/armor details at fixed human scale, but remount them on mechanically connected radial clamps/hinged hangers. Add a distinct spindle and bearing ring, segmented geared base, sloped inspection trays and an actually recessed vise. Separate graphite steel, pale serial plates and muted brass clamp edges; avoid emissive inventory/reward-like icons. Any new task lighting should be local and separately visually reviewed. Preserve the real equipment reward interaction and its brighter readable affordance.

## Tests and acceptance sequence

1. Preserve the current four-island baseline and its successful encounter evidence as historical; add new topology tests first. Snapshot/fingerprint all nineteen other templates and shared gameplay files so only Armory changes. Assert unchanged spawn/exit/breaches and route/reward timing.
2. Test production occupancy and swept navigation through the mouth, between counter and carousel, and around the entire outer retreat loop for the player and the actual largest Armory enemy radius (including elite variants). Include negative cross-counter/carousel segments and legal axis-slide controls. Confirm shots block on solid counter/carousel regions and clear intended gaps; do not lower actor radii or weaken shared traversal assertions.
3. Test mesh/collision agreement before and after baking, full mesh extents, supported spindle/clamps/counter connections, fixed rifle dimensions, visibility rays to key mechanism parts through the open sector, disposal and actual material/draw-call budgets. Radial clamp tests should check differing angular placements/orientations around the spindle, not merely object names. Adapt old four-name/rectangle assertions only as an explicit new layout contract, retaining rifle/armor readability and resource-ownership checks where applicable.
4. Obtain same-camera native entry, mouth, flank and overview views against the current-main baseline. Independently assess whether spindle/gears/radial clamps read as a rotary mechanism and whether the counter forms a horseshoe around it. Tests cannot decide that visual question.
5. Run the actual desktop elite encounter with native controls through mouth and outer routes, equipment reward selection and onward transition. Validate source hashes, video, all actor movement segments where snapshot chords are ambiguous, viewport/ammo/status clearance and reward legibility. Include desktop resize and keyboard access; no phone work. Preserve failed attempts, run required desktop verification/CI and review the final integration tree.

The issue explicitly allows support posts on existing islands or a future approved horseshoe; it does not make the current islands an adequate horseshoe by declaration. This report proposes that new bounded layout for implementation and review, but grants no human art acceptance and does not close the original brief. No source edits, tests or renderer launches were performed.
