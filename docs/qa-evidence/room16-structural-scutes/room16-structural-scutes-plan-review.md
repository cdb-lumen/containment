# Room16 structural scute plan review

The proposed direction is a concrete structural change consistent with the brief, and suitable for a bounded prototype. It is not visual acceptance or a guarantee that seven to nine pieces will read biologically. Keep the20c three-root organization and150-point contour; that organization is required, not the reason for rejection. Preserve rejected20c/58b2 and the earlier rectangular-overlay captures.

The key distinction from the rejected four rectangular overlays is construction: several broad unequal curved shell volumes should visibly overlap with a common growth direction and exposed, connected underlying tissue at selected breaks. They must modify the mass hierarchy rather than sit as isolated plates on an otherwise unchanged smooth platform. Seven to nine is a working budget, not a successful-appearance criterion. Use a few large readable relationships at native scale, rather than multiplying thin lines.

Specific mechanism risks and controls:

- Retain a continuous sealed base through the .95-world-unit shot plane. Carapace breaks must expose real underlying tissue, not introduce visible walkable-looking holes above a collider or violate the sealed silhouette. Preserve the expanded pre/post ray grid, all old route/anchor checks and explicit historical footprint deltas.
- Scutes need real support/overlap and thickness. Avoid coplanar seams, floating rear edges, repeated manufactured bevels and a uniform flat rim. Validate named shell→tendon/base contact chains and native-direction first-hit exposure of both shell and selected underlying breaks. Every vertex, triangle midpoint/centroid and whole-model height remains inside the contour/44.8 budget; samples remain finite, so inspect concave-boundary crossings carefully.
- Enlarged cut ends should be cross-sections of the attached root volume: an interrupted outer sheath continuing into the adjacent shell, visibly recessed/uneven internal tissue and a meaningful edge reveal. Avoid another light top polygon surrounded by a tiny border. Preserve named cut first-hit rays from the shipping camera direction as well as top views, and ensure the southern cut is not swallowed by the new crown.
- Make cabinet consumption a connected side-to-roof relationship while preserving a coherent human casing, named ports and torn lid. The existing cabinet roof is already near43 units, leaving less than2 units before44.8; raising a thick new roof cover is likely to violate the budget or hide hardware. Prefer a supported side wrap/tongue climbing onto a selected roof corner, with sufficient exposed front/roof area, rather than burying the whole box or lowering it blindly into the body. Require actual contact/intersection and first-hit rays at specific surviving human features and engulfing features.
- Keep the cavity's dark recess open and readable; added scutes must not form a camera-facing hood. Test multiple cavity witnesses and approach-direction rays. Avoid bright eye/beam/telegraph cues.
- Reuse the existing seven material identities and owned geometry lifecycle. New closed pieces need manifold/winding/positive-volume coverage and append/bake/dispose verification; no shared-geometry mutation or new global lights, camera changes, timers or gameplay changes.

The structural proposal addresses the unresolved visual causes more directly than another silhouette/color/cap-size change. Before a full encounter, inspect matched native center/north and overview images: do shell and exposed tissue read as different connected layers, do tips read as severed roots, and does a recognizably human cabinet appear physically consumed? If they still read as isolated plates/caps on a molded manifold, stop and preserve that failure instead of passing it through CPU tests. Full ordinary encounter and emergence/route readability remain separate later gates; no human-approval requirement is invented here.
