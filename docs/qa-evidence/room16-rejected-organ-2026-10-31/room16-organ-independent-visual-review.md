# Room16 organic major-form comparison — independent visual review

Verdict: the original art criterion remains unmet. Candidate `91f60c8309577592b077750a8b7bf23496df649d` is a technically bounded revision, but I would not qualify it as an alien organic growth visibly consuming a distinct human distributor. The earlier major-form rejection remains unresolved.

## Findings

- **P2 — manufactured Y-platform reading persists** (`src/render/SwarmJunction.ts:66–88`). In both native views and the fitted overview, the candidate retains two long, straight, nearly symmetric arms, squared terminations, a straight southern stem and an uninterrupted regular perimeter. The warm surface and rounded underside change the finish and edge shading, but the dominant silhouette still reads as a constructed Y trough/platform. The internal folds read as a lining laid into that structure. They do not establish an irregular autonomous growth engulfing equipment.
- **P2 — distinct consumed human distributor remains insufficiently legible** (`src/render/SwarmJunction.ts:119–125`). The pale enclosure fragments are more visible than the baseline blue-gray pieces. However, the exposed angular corner/lid occupies a small part of the central circular opening and can read as a rim fitting or patch belonging to the same Y apparatus. Across the supplied views, it does not establish a recognizable separate human distribution device with organic mass overtaking it. Material contrast is improved; the required relationship between the two objects remains ambiguous.

A further revision needs stronger major-form separation, not merely another color adjustment. Unequal masses, variable thickness and visibly irregular shell contours could interrupt the platform reading while retaining the sealed collision contract; coherent enclosure remnants need enough shape and mechanical detail to identify the human device independently. Those are directions for a new candidate, not approval of an unrendered solution.
