# Room16 lobed native independent visual review

Verdict: visual qualification remains FAILED for the biological construction/consumed-hub brief. The150-point prototype makes a genuine, visible silhouette change, but does not yet resolve the central art rejection. This is not a requirement to erase the three-root/Y organization: three service-direction roots converging on one routing organ are explicitly required. The problem is how that mass is constructed and read at shipping scale.

## Actual visual findings

1. **P2, layered biological construction still missing.** All three new views show a broad, continuous grey-green surface and a nearly uniform extruded edge. Lobed perimeter and uneven shoulders soften the former narrow Y, but the dominant read remains a cast/molded manifold or platform. The brief's overlapping charcoal carapace over distinguishable ribs/tendon is not legible. The outline is improved; smooth shading over one mass does not provide that required construction hierarchy.
2. **P2, terminal tissue still reads as end caps.** The enlarged west/east/south pale patches are now more visible and separately identifiable, which is an improvement. However, each is still a light, relatively flat inset at a root tip with a narrow dark border. At native scale these read as plugs/caps or decorative inserts rather than exposed internal cross-sections of a thick severed root. Increasing their footprint alone has not resolved the material/volume relationship.
3. **P2, consumption of the human hub remains weak.** The pale cabinet is identifiable as an angular manufactured component, and its dark panel/slot helps distinguish it from the organ. But it sits cleanly on the upper-right surface; the engulfing fold and conduit relationship do not read clearly enough to show the organ taking over the distributor. At overview size the cabinet becomes a pale box perched on the mass. Actual geometry contact/exposure tests do not establish the consuming gesture visually.

The dark recessed cavity is clear in the new native views and retains a bounded low rim; it does not look like a bright eye or new attack telegraph. Three root directions, damaged far-wall gantry, unlit organ and canonical objective remain apparent. The marine is visible at both staged poses. No obvious new floating part, GPU corruption or HUD collision is evident in these static views. These positives do not close the art findings above.

## Scope and next decision

The source-backed room brief requires three long roots, distinct cut ends, overlapping carapace/rib/tendon construction and a recognizable severed human distributor being consumed. It does not require an arbitrary non-Y composition or a new boss/hive mechanic. Preserve the20c capture as the result of this major silhouette experiment rather than relabeling it accepted because topology/tests passed.

Do not continue small perimeter, color or cap-size tweaks expecting them to solve the same rejection. Any further candidate should first make a substantial, inspectable change to the structural relationships: readable overlapping shell volumes with exposed underlying tissue at selected breaks, cut faces that visibly belong to root volume, and growth crossing/engulfing a meaningful part of the cabinet while enough coherent human hardware remains exposed. Keep the established height/collision/route budgets and rerun geometry controls for such changes. No change is implemented or approved by this advice.

This review contains no ordinary encounter, dynamic approach, enemy-emergence, dense-horde readability, performance, historical delivery or human-approval claim. Native encounter has not run. Prior rejected prototypes and this rejection remain separate from CPU correctness.
