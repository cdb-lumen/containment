## Room purpose and occupants

This is an occupied passenger cryogenic storage and servicing room. It keeps sleeping travellers alive during transit, with staff access for inspection and maintenance. The player has just left their own awakening bay and now sees that the ship carries other people worth saving. Do not depict corpses, exposed mannequins or abandoned tombs. Passenger identities, total capacity and pod arrangement are not established by the campaign.
