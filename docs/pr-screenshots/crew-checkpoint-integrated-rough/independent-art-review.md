## Visual findings

The paired blue shield lines and attached tan-topped northern station form a clear fortified bay. Their shared alignment makes the central structure read as one checkpoint rather than scattered furniture. The open southern mouth and broad outside lanes remain legible across the sheets. Actors provide a usable scale reference, and the native guard-bay image clearly places the actor inside the enclosure. No obvious floating or intersecting room geometry warrants rejecting the rough in this coverage.

The room is still sparse. The shield faces dominate the composition but read as long, thin panels with repeated diagonal strips. The counter has recognizable equipment masses, while their individual functions remain weak at phone scale. These are unfinished model-quality issues, not reasons to restart the layout.

The expanded phone story panel obscures the upper portion of the actor in the north image. The ammo card also covers the lower right shield end in both native images. The skipped-story sheet exposes more of the northern approach. Record this as a shared HUD visibility dependency, not a room-local geometry defect. Do not move the checkpoint to evade it or edit HUD, camera or AI. Occluded areas are not visually certified by this review.

The later CPU report supports the visible circulation reading: tested access enters from the south, with northern approaches detouring around either side. The counter blocks a straight northern crossing. Those are reported CPU results, not tests rerun here. They do not establish complete visual/collision parity or final art acceptance.
