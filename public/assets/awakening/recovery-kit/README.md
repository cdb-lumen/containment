# Recovery kit art subset

Original console, medical drawers and south-facing recovery chair, authored for the frozen 61153a9 monitoring/recovery reservations. This is an asset-only subset, not a complete room or complete kit. Personal locker, satellite folding cabinet and trolley remain on the parent checklist.

## Reproduce

From repository root, with Blender 4.0.2, its NumPy dependency, Pillow and DejaVu Sans Mono installed:

```sh
python3 scripts/create-kit-display.py
blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 -t 6 -P scripts/build-recovery-kit.py
blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 -t 2 -P scripts/verify-recovery-kit.py
```

`recovery-kit.blend` retains named editable parts and packed images. `recovery-kit.glb` is batched by material and contains no studio floor, lights or camera. Studio PNGs are rendered from actual imported GLB, not the source scene. Source depends on the approved `public/assets/benchmark/sealed-cryo.blend` already in this commit's ancestry.

## Materials and licensing

Original geometry and source scripts are project-authored under the repository license. Medical enamel, graphite, satin stainless and seals reuse the approved chamber's packed original texture atlases and PBR definitions. Clean atlas regions avoid mapping unrelated chamber-specific wear patches onto small kit parts. Only short modeled pull-contact abrasions are new wear. The LCD atlas is original raster artwork generated by the included script, using system DejaVu type. No corpse, occupants, loose props or invented lore.

There are no baked normal maps. Bevels are geometry; normal vectors are exported and checked. Upholstery and ochre use scalar PBR. Shared atlas images are embedded in this standalone GLB, not automatically GPU-deduplicated with another loaded GLB. The parent must decide shared texture ownership at integration.
