# Status flipbooks

Flame02.png and WispySmoke02.png are lossless PNG conversions of Unity Labs / Thomas Iche CC0 flipbooks.

Source and license declaration: https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/free-vfx-image-sequences-flipbooks
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original archives:
- https://unity3d.com/files/labs/downloads/vfx/assets01/Flame02/Flame02-flipbooks.zip
- https://unity3d.com/files/labs/downloads/vfx/assets01/WispySmoke02/WispySmoke02-flipbooks.zip

Original atlases: Flame02_16x4.tga, 2048x1024, 64 frames; WispySmoke02_8x8.tga, 1024x1024, 64 frames. No resampling. Frost accumulation shader and fractured ice geometry authored for Containment; no third-party ice assets.
