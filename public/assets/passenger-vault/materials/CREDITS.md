# Material layer credits

Original deterministic procedural texture maps and material assignments authored by Hermes Agent for the Containment project under Viktor's direction. `tools/assets/passenger-vault/materials.py` defines the texture pixels and UV assignments. No photographs, downloads, manufacturer maps, external normal maps or third-party texture assets are included.

The six variant GLBs reuse the accepted original `chamber.glb` and `row-carrier.glb` geometry from the parent directory without changing positions, normals, indices or scene nodes. Their construction credits remain in `../CREDITS.md`; its geometry-only description does not describe this later material layer.

These maps, variant assignments and editable review scene use the project's MIT license, `LICENSE`, copyright 2026 Lumen. No CC0 dedication, manufacturer endorsement, medical certification or separate third-party asset license is asserted. Blender and Three.js are tools, not sources for the texture artwork.

Reproduction commands and limits are in `tools/assets/passenger-vault/materials/README.md`. The material manifest records source geometry hashes, final generator hash, encoded file hashes and decoded RGB map hashes. `tools/assets/passenger-vault/materials/recovery/provenance.json` records the exact editable Blender scene, scene-import script and import receipt hashes.

This is a bounded unintegrated material candidate. Wear, complete role identification, whole-room material budgets and independent current-pixel acceptance remain open.
